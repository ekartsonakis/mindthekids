# MindTheKids — Παιχνίδια Μυαλού 🌱

**MindTheKids** is a calm, offline-first Progressive Web App (PWA) with **18 cognitive & brain-training games** designed for **preschool children and older**, available in **Greek (`ΕΛ`)** and **English (`EN`)**.

---

## 🌿 Philosophy & Benefits (Γιατί είναι ιδανικό για παιδιά & γονείς)

- **Cognitive Growth**: Exercises memory, visual & selective attention, logical reasoning, spatial perception, musical ear, and early math skills across progressive difficulty levels (`Easy`, `Medium`, `Hard`, `Expert`).
- **Safe, Calm, Low-Stimulation Environment**: No ads, no tracking, no stressful countdown timers to lose, no hyper-stimulating animations, and no addictive "gamification" loops.
- **100% Offline / Airplane Mode Ready**: Once opened in a browser, the Service Worker (`/sw.js`) caches all code, SVG illustrations, and voice audio locally so parents can switch the tablet or phone to **Airplane Mode** and hand it to their child completely offline.
- **Bilingual Spoken Audio**: Includes pre-bundled natural Greek (`/audio/el/*.mp3`) and English (`/audio/en/*.mp3`) voice prompts for **Flags of the World**, ensuring crystal-clear pronunciation across all browsers (Chrome, Opera, Edge, Firefox, Safari, Android, iOS) even without OS TTS language packs.
- **Light & Dark Mode**: Warm Montessori cream palette for daytime and a cozy low-glare dark mode for evening play.

## 🧩 The 18 Brain Games

### 🧠 Memory & Music (Μνήμη & Μουσική)
1. **Memory Pairs (Ζευγάρια Μνήμης)** — Match pairs of cards (`6`, `12`, `16`, or `20` cards).
2. **Sequence Memory (Μνήμη Ακολουθίας)** — Watch and repeat growing color sequences (`4`, `6`, or `8` steps).
3. **Melody Memory (Μουσική Ακολουθία)** — Listen to discreet, gentle music-box notes (`Do, Re, Mi, Fa, Sol, La`) and build real children's melodies (*Twinkle Twinkle Little Star / Φεγγαράκι μου λαμπρό*, *Lightly Row / Χαρωπά τα δυο μου χέρια*, *Ode to Joy / Ωδή στη Χαρά*) note by note (`5`, `8`, or `14+` notes).
4. **Flags of the World (Σημαίες του Κόσμου)** — Listen to the spoken country name in Greek or English and match the SVG flag (`2`, `4`, or `6` flags at once).

### 👀 Attention (Προσοχή & Παρατήρηση)
5. **Target Tap (Βρες τον Στόχο)** — Find and tap all target items among distractors (`4×3`, `4×4`, or `5×5` grids).
6. **Gentle Mole (Το Τυφλοποντικάκι)** — Calm garden mole game with adjustable speeds (`Easy`, `Medium`, `Hard`) and flower decoys, with no stressful game-over timer.
7. **Schulte Number Grid (Πίνακας Schulte)** — Visual scanning tables in `2×2` (1–4), `3×3` (1–9), `4×4` (1–16), and classic `5×5` (1–25).
8. **Picture Mahjong (Ματζόνγκ Εικόνων)** — Layered tile matching (`10`, `14`, or `22` tiles across up to 3 layers).

### 🔢 Math & Numbers (Αριθμοί & Μαθηματικά)
9. **Number Match (Ταίριασμα Αριθμών)** — Match identical numbers, pairs making `5`, pairs making `10` (with counting dots), or `Hard 10` (`5×4` grid without dots).
10. **Picture Math (Μαθηματικά με Εικόνες)** — Visual Counting, Addition (`+`), Subtraction (`-`), and Mixed Operations up to `20`.

### 🧩 Logic & Sudoku (Λογική & Σχεδιασμός)
11. **Color & Shape Sudoku (Χρωματιστό Σουντόκου)** — `3×3`, `4×4`, and `6×6` Sudoku using distinct pastel colors and shape icons.
12. **Marble Jump (Παιχνίδι με Μπίλιες / Peg Solitaire)** — Jump marbles to leave only 1 marble on the board (`3`, `5`, `9`, or `15` marbles) with Undo & Restart.
13. **Pattern Train (Το Τρενάκι των Μοτίβων)** — Complete logical sequences (`ABAB`, `AAB`, `ABC`, `ABAC`, `ABCD`) on the train wagons.
14. **Odd One Out (Βρες το Διαφορετικό)** — Spot the item that differs by category, color, shape, or orientation (`4`, `6`, or `9` cards).
15. **Rainbow Rings Tower (Πύργος των Κρίκων / Tower of Hanoi)** — Move `3`, `4`, or `5` rainbow rings to the target peg.

### 📐 Shapes & Space (Σχήματα & Χώρος)
16. **Shape Rotation (Περιστροφή Σχημάτων)** — Identify which asymmetric 2D shape matches the target when rotated (`90°/180°/270°` or diagonal `45°` increments).
17. **Shadows & Sorting (Σκιές & Ταξινόμηση)** — Match objects to their silhouettes (`3` or `6` shadows) or sort items into category baskets.
18. **Copy the Pattern (Αντίγραψε το Σχέδιο)** — Reproduce pixel patterns on `3×3`, `4×4`, and `5×5` grids.
